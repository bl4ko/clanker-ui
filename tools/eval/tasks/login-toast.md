Build the sign-in page of an app called Atlas.

- Email and password fields with validation messages, a Remember me checkbox, a Forgot password link, and a Sign in button.
- A valid submit shows a success toast "Signed in" and stays on the page. The email `locked@example.com` shows an error toast that the account is locked.
- It must work and look good on a phone and on a desktop, in light and dark mode.
