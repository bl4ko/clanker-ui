Build the settings page of a team workspace app called Relay.

- Profile: display name, email, and a short bio, with a Save button.
- Notifications: switches for email, push, and a weekly digest; a select for the digest day (Monday to Friday) that is disabled while the digest is off. Save button.
- Danger zone: a Delete workspace button that asks for confirmation first.
- Each section saves on its own and shows a toast. Invalid input shows a message under the field.
- It must work and look good on a phone and on a desktop, in light and dark mode.
