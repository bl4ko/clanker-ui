Introduction

shadcn/ui for plain HTML.

All 65 shadcn/ui components as three files you copy into any project: one stylesheet, one ES module, one chart module. No build step, no npm, no CDN. Behavior comes from HTML attributes and runs under a strict Content Security Policy with Trusted Types.

Browse components Theming
  • Three filesCopy them, never edit them. Put app styles in your own app.css.
  • Strict CSPNo innerHTML, no inline script or style. Trusted Types enforced.
  • DeclarativeMarkup mirrors shadcn: data-slot, data-variant, data-size, data-state.

Quick start

<link rel="stylesheet" href="clanker-ui.css">
<link rel="stylesheet" href="app.css">
<script src="clanker-ui-theme.js"></script>
<script type="module" src="clanker-ui.js"></script>

The Lighthouse Keeper

A short story about a keeper who measured the sea every night and wrote everything down.

The Logbook

Each evening the keeper climbed the spiral stair, lit the lamp, and wrote the wind, the swell, and the ships that passed into a heavy logbook.

"Write down what you see, not what you expect to see," the old keeper said, "and the sea will teach you the rest."

What the Keeper Recorded

The entries were short and always in the same order:

  • Wind direction and force on the Beaufort scale
  • Swell height measured against the north rock
  • Every light seen on the horizon after dusk

Reading the Data

Years later, a student ran grep -c storm logbook.txt on the transcribed pages and found a pattern nobody had noticed.

DecadeStorm nightsShips seen
1890s2124,810
1900s1875,233
1910s2413,902

The keeper never knew. The logbook did.
