Build the invoices page of a billing app called Tally.

- A table of 25 sample invoices: number, customer, amount, status (draft, sent, paid, overdue), and due date.
- A text filter, a status filter, sorting by amount and by due date, and paging with 10 rows per page.
- Each row has an actions menu. Edit opens a dialog with a form for the customer, the amount, and the status; Save updates the row and shows a toast. Delete removes the row.
- It must work and look good on a phone and on a desktop, in light and dark mode.
